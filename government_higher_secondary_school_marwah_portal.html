From the Principal's Desk

Message from the Principal

It gives me immense pleasure to welcome students, parents, educators, and visitors to the official digital portal of Government Higher Secondary School Marwah. Located in the picturesque and resilient Marwah valley of District Kishtwar, our institution stands as a beacon of higher secondary education and holistic student development.

Our mission is to bridge geographical challenges with academic excellence, scientific curiosity, moral integrity, and digital empowerment. Together with our dedicated faculty and supportive community, we strive to ensure every student realizes their highest potential.

Dr. Gopal Krishan

Principal, GHSS Marwah (UDISE: 01170201315)
